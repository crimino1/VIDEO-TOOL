DESCRIPTION
The entire Calculus 1 course in one video, start to finish. Limits, derivatives, all the applications, integrals and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: everything a normal first semester of calculus (and AP Calculus AB) covers, explained simply on a chalkboard with clear animations.

How to use this video: don't watch it all in one sitting. Watch a chapter or two, take a break, and come back to exactly where you stopped using the chapters below. Keep a notebook open, and when I ask you to try one yourself, pause and actually try it. It finishes with a 16-question final exam that tells you which chapter to review.

You only need basic algebra (equations, fractions, exponents). Chapter 1 is a warm-up that reviews everything you need.
