Second anatomy-figure workout video (2026-11-10), built unattended. Reused chair-workout/blender/um_auto.blend; six new core poses were added to chair-workout/blender/chair_ex.py (side_bend, cross_crunch, knee_tuck, flutter_kicks, twist_punch, woodchopper); four loops were reused from chair-workout frames. tools/make_clips.py and edit/build.py follow the chair-workout layout and add a green wipe into each set, countdown ring bursts, form-tip pills, a HALFWAY banner, an intro with a live looping figure and an outro checklist.

Music: vidIQ had 1 credit and HyperFrames bgm needs the heygen CLI, so music came from Openverse/Jamendo (Marco Margna, CC BY-SA 3.0, credits in belly-workout/music/CREDITS.txt), crossfaded into music/bed.wav at -16 LUFS, played at 0.16 volume.

**Why:** the user asked for good background music without being asked anything; free CC music with credit was the only source available.
**How to apply:** for later workout videos reuse this music method (search Openverse, license_type=commercial, pick instrumental/energetic by tags) unless vidIQ credits are back. Related: [[chair-workout-project]], [[health-voice-api]].
