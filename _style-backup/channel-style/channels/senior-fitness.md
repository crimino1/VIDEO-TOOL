## Shots and camera
- Stock: slow push-in from 100% to 105% across the shot `[measured]`.
- 3D: full body in frame, from a 3/4 angle for seated moves and from behind for moves holding the chair. Keep the part being taught visible, such as heels for heel raises `[seen]`.
- Hold the starting position until the voice cues the move, then repeat the move until the section ends `[measured]`.
- Show the feet close-up for heel-to-toe walking `[seen 6:16]`.

## Transitions
Straight cut everywhere `[seen]`. Text fades and slides in; nothing else moves between shots.

## Do and don't
**Do**
- Show the safety note before any exercise `[seen]`.
- Match every rep count and cue on screen to the voice `[seen]`.
- Show the movement exactly as the voice describes it, including which leg `[seen]`.

**Don't**
- Don't use footage from YouTube or Pinterest without checking the rights, because of the channel's demonetization.
- Don't show people on the floor or using mats when the voice says no floor work. The class shot at 0:10 has mats and should be replaced next time `[seen 0:13]`.
- Don't put text over the feet, hands or face in an exercise `[seen]`.
- Don't use tiny text or fast cuts.
