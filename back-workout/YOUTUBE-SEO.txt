TITLE
Chair Exercises for Back Pain Relief | 10 Minute Seated Stretches for Seniors

(shorter option: Seated Stretches for Back Pain | 10 Min Chair Routine)

DESCRIPTION
Gentle seated stretches to ease back pain and stiffness, in a calm 10-minute follow-along chair routine. Good for seniors, beginners and anyone who sits for long hours. Nine easy moves for your lower back, middle back, shoulders and hips, with no floor work and no equipment.

An on-screen spine meter shows when to stretch and when to relax, and a timer counts every set.

Each stretch: 45 seconds, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

STRETCHES
0:00 Intro: before you start
0:30 1. Seated Shoulder Rolls
1:30 2. Seated Cat-Cow
2:30 3. Seated Torso Twist
3:30 4. Seated Side Reach
4:30 5. Seated Pelvic Tilt
5:30 6. Seated Knee Hug
6:30 7. Seated Forward Fold
7:30 8. Seated Hamstring Stretch
8:30 9. Seated Chest Opener
9:30 Routine complete!

Do these stretches every day, morning or evening. Sit tall on a sturdy chair without wheels. Move slowly, breathe deeply and only stretch to a gentle pull, never into pain. If you have severe, sudden or worsening back pain, numbness, or a back injury, see your doctor before exercising.
