DESCRIPTION
A gentle 13-minute Tai Chi routine for seniors and beginners. Ten slow, flowing moves to improve balance, loosen stiff joints, build easy leg strength and calm your mind. Every move is done standing in place with soft knees.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The working muscles glow, an on-screen balance meter shows which side leads, and a timer counts every move: 60 seconds of flow, then 12 seconds to see the next move. Breathe slowly, keep the moves small, and never force a position.

Practise a few minutes every day. Keep a chair within reach for the one-leg moves, and lift your knee only as high as feels steady. If you have balance problems, dizziness or a recent injury, check with your doctor first.
