DESCRIPTION
A gentle 7-minute morning stretch for seniors and beginners to ease stiff joints, wake up your body and start the day moving well. Follow along from your chair: 8 easy stretches, five seated and three standing, no equipment needed.

This daily morning routine loosens your shoulders, back, hips and ankles. An on-screen stretch meter shows when to reach and when to relax, and a timer counts every move: 40 seconds of stretching, then 10 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Good for: older adults, beginners, morning stiffness, arthritis-friendly gentle movement, and anyone over 50, 60 or 70 who wants an easy daily stretching habit.

CHAPTERS
0:00 Good morning: before you start
0:30 1. Seated Overhead Reach
1:20 2. Gentle Torso Twist
2:10 3. Ankle Circles
3:00 4. Seated Cat-Cow
3:50 5. Shoulder Rolls
4:40 6. Sit to Stand
5:30 7. Standing Side Bend
6:20 8. Easy Standing March
7:10 All done, have a great day!

Save this video and do it every morning. Move slowly, breathe deeply, and never bounce or push into pain. Hold the back of a chair for the standing moves if you need to. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
