TITLE
What Would Happen If You Fell Into The Mariana Trench

DESCRIPTION
You're floating 300 km southwest of Guam, and you let go. Fall with us through every zone of the ocean, past glowing anglerfish, the deepest fish ever filmed and the last of the light, all the way to the bottom of Challenger Deep, 11 km down.

The falling diver is an animation made from real diver photographs; the journey is imagined, the animals and facts are real.
