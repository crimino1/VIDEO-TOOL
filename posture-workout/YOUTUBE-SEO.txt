DESCRIPTION
Posture exercises for seniors to fix a hunched back, rounded shoulders and forward head posture. Follow along in this 15-minute routine to stand taller and ease neck and upper back strain: 12 gentle exercises, eleven done sitting in a chair and one standing. No equipment needed.

These moves strengthen the upper back, open a tight chest and retrain your neck to sit over your shoulders. An on-screen posture meter shows when to work and when to relax, and a timer counts every set: 55 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Good for: slouching, kyphosis, rounded shoulders, text neck, desk posture, and older adults over 50, 60 or 70 who want to stand straighter.

Do this routine every day for the best results. Move slowly, keep breathing, and stop if anything hurts. If you have osteoporosis, a spine condition or a recent injury, check with your doctor or physiotherapist before starting.
