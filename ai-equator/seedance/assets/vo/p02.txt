Quick background. Seedance 2.0 came out in February and went viral, and Hollywood was not happy. Disney, Paramount and the Motion Picture Association all sent ByteDance legal letters over copyrighted characters. Then Seedance 2.5 launched on July 31st, in Dreamina and ByteDance's own apps. And this month, it got two upgrades. A 4K version, which opened to developers on November 17th, after a trial with 20 studios. And Seedance 2.5 Flash, a faster and cheaper version, on November 15th.
