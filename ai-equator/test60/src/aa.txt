Comparison Summary

Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Gemini 4 Carbon (High) scores 60 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 37). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 205M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 72.6M.

Pricing for Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $7.76 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Carbon (High) on the Intelligence Index.

At 30.7 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is notably slow compared with the median of 12.8 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
