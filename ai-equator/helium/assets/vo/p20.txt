And Google has the hardware to run that loop. Its eighth generation TPUs, announced in April, come in two versions, one for training and one for inference. A single training pod has 9,600 chips and 121 exaflops, almost three times Ironwood. Google is spending around 180 billion dollars on infrastructure this year. The Gemini app just passed a billion monthly users. Google Cloud revenue more than doubled, and Google's own model APIs now handle around 32 billion tokens every minute. Google has even launched TPUs into space, to test running AI in orbit.
