DESCRIPTION
Seven months ago people were asking if Google had lost the AI race: no new top-tier Gemini model since February, Gemini 3.5 Pro cancelled, a DeepMind shake-up, and OpenAI and Anthropic shipping again and again. Then came Gemini 4. In about seven weeks Google released four models (Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen and Neon), briefly took #1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, put Hydrogen inside Google Search for everyone, and now has two of the four smartest models on that leaderboard. Here's how the comeback happened, what each model does, how they stack up against OpenAI and Anthropic, the catch in Neon's price, what Google's own safety report says, and whether Google is really back.
