DESCRIPTION
Thinking Machines (Mira Murati's lab) released Inkling 2 Mini on November 20, 2026: an open-weights, 26.4B-parameter looped mixture-of-experts model (3.4B active) that runs on a 32 GB Mac. Its middle 12 layers run three times per token, so it gets 48 layer passes from 24 stored layers. In this video: what it is and how the loop works, Thinking Machines' own demos, real tests from people who ran it (agent harness builds, a blind test against Qwen where Inkling won 2 of 3 rounds, coding, vision, and where it broke), measured tokens per second on Mac mini, MacBook, Mac Studio, RTX 3060/3090/4060/4090/5090 and DGX Spark, prefill and memory, the benchmarks against Qwen 3.8 27B, Gemma 4 31B, Muse Glimmer and Nemotron, cost per task, and who should and shouldn't use it.

Honest note: my editing laptop has 16 GB of RAM, so I didn't run it myself. Every test shown is credited on screen and linked below. Speeds are community measurements, not official numbers. The 210 tok/s figure is Exo Labs' (launch partner) claim. Vals' own page shows 44.03%, not the 46.5% Thinking Machines quotes.
