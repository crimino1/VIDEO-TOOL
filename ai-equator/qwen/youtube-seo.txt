DESCRIPTION
Alibaba just launched Qwen4-Max and calls it a frontier model. On output it costs about 9x less than Claude Fable 5.2 ($4.50 vs $40 per million tokens). So is it actually that good, or is the price the only frontier thing about it? I go through what it is, what it costs, Alibaba's own hand-picked benchmark chart (and who's missing from it), the independent scores from Artificial Analysis and Vals AI, and what happened when real testers put it to work: Atomic Agent's 59-test coding job, Kingy AI's 200-stage study, MindStudio's hands-on builds and a 6-round chip benchmark run on X. Then the problems, and my verdict.
