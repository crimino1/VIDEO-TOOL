MIT researchers handed GPT-6 Astra the weights of another AI and told it: make this better at math. No retraining, no new data. Astra found a looping bug in two layers of Qwen3.5-0.8B, changed 0.016% of its weights by hand, and MATH-500 went from 7.9% to 48.1%. But there's a twist most coverage skipped. Here's what happened, why it matters, and what the critics found.
