DESCRIPTION
MIT researchers handed GPT-6 Astra the weights of another AI and said: make this better at math. No retraining, no new data. Astra found a looping bug in two layers of Qwen3.5-0.8B, changed just 0.016% of its weights by hand, and its MATH-500 score jumped from 7.9% to 48.1%. It also named which game a network was trained on just from its numbers, and hand-wrote a Breakout network that beat one trained for 100 million rounds. But there's a twist most coverage skipped: critics reran the untouched model with better settings and got close to the edited score. Here's what really happened, why it matters, and what it means for AI improving AI.
