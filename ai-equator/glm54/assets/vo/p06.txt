Z.ai calls it their first Loop Transformer. They didn't train it from scratch, either: they took GLM-5, already trained, and taught it to loop. It's about 750 billion parameters, and they claim it thinks like a model with two to three trillion. It runs up to two hundred tokens a second off-peak, seventy to eighty when it's busy. And they admit one flaw themselves: sometimes it gets stuck in what they call marathon thinking.
