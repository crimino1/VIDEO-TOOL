DESCRIPTION
Z.ai (Zhipu AI) released GLM-5.4 on November 23, 2026, with no launch event and no benchmark chart, just one post on X. It's their first "Loop Transformer": instead of adding parameters, it runs 28 layers of GLM-5 twice for every token. That makes it deeper without making it bigger. In this video: who Zhipu is, the GLM release pace this year, what's new in 5.4 (vision, a 1M-token context, 50% more Coding Plan quota), how the loop works, the one test Z.ai did publish (a 30B test model), what's still missing (benchmarks, API, open weights), the official demos, how to try it, and what's coming next.

Note: Z.ai has not published GLM-5.4 benchmark scores. The +11.3 AIME number in this video is from their 30B test model, not GLM-5.4. The "matches 2–3T models" line is Z.ai's own claim. The December date for the next model comes from a single report and is unconfirmed.
