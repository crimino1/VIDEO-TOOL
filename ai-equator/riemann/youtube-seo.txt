DESCRIPTION
On Friday, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge posted a four-page note: "I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis." He says the same method proves the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. The written-out version ran past 250,000 pages, so he released the proof only in Lean: 17,374,215 lines that a computer can check step by step. I break down what was claimed, what Claude actually contributed, what Terence Tao and other mathematicians are saying, how the independent checks are going, and the twist: the same day, a fully human 940-page proposed proof by Alessandro Masullo appeared on Ravi Vakil's blog. Plus the skeptical side, why the Clay Institute still lists it as unsolved, and what happens next.
