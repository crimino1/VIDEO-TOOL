DESCRIPTION
I gave GPT-6.2 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2 the exact same 6,194-character prompt: build a photorealistic, real-time 3D simulation of our solar system in one HTML file, with a living Sun, every planet, Saturn's rings with real shadows, 15,000 asteroids, comets and real Kepler orbits. Astra ran in OpenAI's Codex app, Fable in Claude Code, both on high. You see both builds, how each model worked (Fable wrote its own checklist and tested in a browser; Astra needed a reminder to stick to one file), the time each took, and a scorecard. Then: what other testers found, the benchmarks where each model wins, cost per task, real-science tests like protein sequences, and the wild things people are building with both.

Honest note: the Codex run started on GPT-6.2 Sol because the wrong model was selected. I switched to Astra partway through, and Astra did the final single-file rebuild you see. No Sol footage is shown.
