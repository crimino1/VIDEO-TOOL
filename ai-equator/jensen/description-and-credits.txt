Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted an affair in a company-wide memo. The woman involved is someone US intelligence reportedly believes was a Chinese intelligence asset, and Nvidia's board has launched an independent investigation. Here's the full story: the spy allegations, the Megaspeed GPU question, the stock, the memes, and what's confirmed versus what's still only alleged.

Nothing in this video claims Jensen Huang or Alice Huang broke any law. The intelligence memo has not been released, no one has been charged, and Nvidia's board says it has no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised. Allegations are labelled as reported.
