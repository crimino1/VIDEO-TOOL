DESCRIPTION
OpenAI just released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. Astra beats Claude Fable 5.2 on Agents' Last Exam at a fraction of the cost per task, tops Artificial Analysis' human-graded 3D test, and it's the first model OpenAI has ever released rated High for AI self-improvement. But early testers are split: Max Weinbach calls it extremely smart, while Theo calls it the worst model OpenAI has ever released. In this review: what's new, the price (and why price per task matters more than price per token), every key benchmark against Fable 5.2 and Gemini 4 Carbon, where it loses, coding, the safety report finding you need to know, what people built, and whether you should use it.
