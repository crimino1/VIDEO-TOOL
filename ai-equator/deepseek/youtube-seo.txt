DESCRIPTION
DeepSeek V5 "leaked", and X lost its mind: V5 beats GPT-6 Astra, V5 drops in November, 2 trillion parameters, trained fully on Chinese chips. I went through every claim. The model names really are in DeepSeek's own GitHub code, but they're test placeholders (one has a 9-token context window). The viral "beats Astra" post had no benchmark, just a logo. The real story is bigger: two Chinese outlets report V5 will be pre-trained on Huawei Ascend 950 chips, likely for a 2027 release. That would be a first for a top Chinese model, and it lands in the same month Nvidia stopped shipping AI chips to China and lost its CEO. Here's what's real, what's rumour, what's made up, and when V5 is actually coming.
