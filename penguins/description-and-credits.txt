TITLE
Why Are So Many Penguins Gay?

DESCRIPTION
In 1999, a male chinstrap penguin at the Central Park Zoo sat on a rock and tried to hatch it, with his partner Roy. Since then, same-sex penguin couples have turned up from Sydney to Berlin, and scientists found the same thing in wild king penguins on Kerguelen. A century earlier, an Antarctic explorer saw it at Cape Adare and was so shocked he wrote his notes in Greek. Why does this one family of birds keep pairing up this way, and is "gay" even the right word?
