BELLY FAT BURN  /  10 min, 50+

DESCRIPTION
A 10-minute belly fat workout for seniors and beginners over 50: five standing moves, then five gentle moves on a mat. It works your belly, waist, hips and deep core with no equipment, and it's kind to your joints.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The worked muscles glow so you can see what each move targets, an on-screen core meter shows when to work, and a timer counts every set: 40 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Exercise burns belly fat best alongside walking, good sleep and sensible eating. Do this routine 3 to 4 times a week.

Keep a sturdy chair close by. Get down to the mat and back up slowly, using the chair for support, and roll onto your side to stand up. Stop if anything hurts. If you have back problems, osteoporosis or a hernia, or have had recent surgery, ask your doctor first.
