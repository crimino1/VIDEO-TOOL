TITLE
10 Min Chair Workout for Seniors | Belly Fat Exercises for Men Over 50

DESCRIPTION
A 10-minute seated core workout for seniors and men over 50. Ten simple chair exercises that target your belly, obliques and lower abs, kind to your back, no floor work and no equipment. The working muscles are shown in red so you know exactly what each move trains.

Each exercise: 42 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

4:12 5. Seated Knee Tuck

8:00 9. Seated Woodchopper
8:57 10. Sit to Stand
9:54 Workout complete!

Do this workout 3 times a week, eat well, and keep moving every day. Go at your own pace, breathe out as you squeeze, and stop if anything hurts. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program.
