CH5 — THE LIGHT CLOCK
Here's the cleanest way to see it. Build a clock out of light.
Two mirrors, one particle of light bouncing between them. Every bounce is one tick.

It's not a broken clock. Time itself ticks slower for the moving ship. This is called time dilation.

Now the real twist. The speed of light isn't really about light.

It's really the speed of cause and effect. The fastest that any change can spread from one place to another.
Here's one popular way to picture it. Think of spacetime: three directions of space, plus one of time.
Everything is always moving through spacetime at the same speed. c.
