Kegel Exercises for Men and Women | 9 Min Pelvic Floor Workout (Follow Along)

(alternative: Kegel Exercises for Men and Women | Strengthen Your Pelvic Floor at Home)

DESCRIPTION
A 9-minute follow-along Kegel workout for men and women. Eight simple pelvic floor exercises you can do sitting on a chair, with an on-screen guide that tells you exactly when to squeeze and when to relax. Good for bladder control, core support and posture, at any age.

How to find your pelvic floor: imagine stopping the flow of urine or holding in gas. That gentle squeeze and lift is a Kegel. Keep breathing, and keep your belly, thighs and buttocks relaxed.

Each exercise: 45 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Do this workout every day. Most people notice a difference in 4 to 6 weeks. Never hold your breath, don't do Kegels while actually urinating, and stop if anything hurts. If you have pelvic pain or a medical condition, check with your doctor or a pelvic floor physiotherapist first.
