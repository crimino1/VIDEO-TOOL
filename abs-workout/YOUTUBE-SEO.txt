TITLE
Easy Chair Abs Workout at Home | 10 Minute Seated Core Exercises for Men, Women, Seniors & Beginners

DESCRIPTION
A 10-minute follow-along chair abs workout you can do at home. Nine easy seated core exercises, no floor work and no equipment, just a sturdy chair. Good for men, women, seniors and beginners. An on-screen core meter shows when to work and when to ease off, and a timer counts every set.

Each exercise: 45 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

EXERCISES
0:00 Intro: before you start
0:30 1. Seated March (warm-up)
1:30 2. Lean-Back Hold
2:30 3. Seated Knee Tuck
3:30 4. Seated Side Bend
4:30 5. Seated Leg Raise
5:30 6. Seated Cross Crunch
6:30 7. Seated Russian Twist
7:30 8. Seated Flutter Kicks
8:30 9. Seated Woodchopper
9:30 Workout complete!

Repeat this workout 3 times a week. Use a sturdy chair without wheels and sit near the front edge. Breathe out on the effort, never hold your breath, and stop if anything hurts. If you have back problems or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
