Being stuck is normal.
It's what math feels like, for everyone.
Can anyone get better at math?
Yes.
People who get good:
– look for the pattern first
– test themselves instead of rereading
– spread practice out and mix it up
– ask why a rule works
– treat being stuck as part of the job
