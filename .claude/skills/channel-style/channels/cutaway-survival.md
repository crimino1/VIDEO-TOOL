## Summary
Vertical 45-70 s YouTube Shorts. A realistic game-engine 3D diorama shown as a **cutaway cross-section** (the ground sliced open like an ant farm) in which "you" (one recurring 3D man) builds a hideout/structure step by step, while an enemy (soldiers, dogs, army) keeps escalating. Second-person narration in a relentless "But… So you…" problem-solution chain at ~230 wpm, one-word captions with yellow keywords, yellow callout tags naming each invention, comic sound-word bursts, and a payoff "Congratulations. You have built a ___."

## Script formula (the core of the channel)
Every build short follows this skeleton `[measured scripts.txt, all 9 build shorts]`:

1. **Hook (0-3 s), condition + danger in one breath:** "If you [hide/sleep/sink] in [ordinary place], [threat happens]." Example pattern: "If you hide in a haystack, soldiers stab it with pitchforks." Voice starts at 0.0 s, no intro, no title card.
2. **Chain of 5-7 loops**, each 5-8 s: "**But** [new threat or flaw]. **So you** [clever low-tech fix]." Enemy tactics escalate in a fixed ladder: spotted → dogs/scent → can't breathe (air vent) → cold/smoke/light gives you away → they stab/probe the ground → they search everywhere.
3. **Named reveal** 2-3 times: "**This is a [term].**" (secret entrance, air vent, light trap, false bottom, moat, thatch roof). Callout tag appears on this line.
4. **Twist (~80% in):** "But one day they search every ___ and soon they'll reach you. So you [move somewhere absurd]… **That's not a mistake.**" Payoff logic: "Nobody searches the water they drink" / "nobody checks the same spot twice" (hide where they already looked / where they depend on).
5. **Cosy wrap (last 8 s):** "Add a bed of ___, a ___, and a [cute animal] who keeps [doing something]." Optional "Just one thing: don't sneeze/don't answer."
6. **Payoff line:** "**Congratulations. You have built a [Y].**" Hard end, no CTA, no subscribe ask.

On-purpose format instead ends mid-sentence ("So until the next flood,") so the last line loops back into the first line `[measured]`. Use only if we test that format.

Counts per build short `[measured, all 9 build scripts]`: "but" 3-8, "so" 4-10, "this is" 0-4 (River 3, Pig pen 2), "Congratulations" 9/9. The twist line is in all 9 but worded freely ("That's not a mistake", "not under the fort by mistake", "That's not a problem"). The 11M and 73K bunker scripts are the densest (8/8 and 7/8 but/so). Sentences 4-12 words. Concrete numbers kept small and physical ("hotter than a hot tub", "as high as a four-story building").

## Pacing
- Voice speed `[measured word count / duration, all 14]`: build format **201-237 wpm** (River 236, Pig pen 233, Jungle 235, Castle 237; Submarine slowest at 201); on-purpose format 167-211. Target ~230. Pauses ≥0.25 s: 0-5 per video, mean 0.32 s (3 shorts measured), so breaths and gaps are cut out.
- Picture pacing has two modes `[measured threshold 0.12]`:
  - **One continuous camera** (River 11M, Pig pen): 0 cuts in 68-71 s. Angle changes come from a slow drifting push and fast camera flights through the diorama, never a cut. Example at 0:46.5-0:51.5: pull back out of the candle room, fly over the river down to water level as soldiers wade, glide back along the bank into the cutaway, ~1.5 s per flight `[seen close/t2, close/t3 at 15 fps]`.
  - **Cut mode** (Well): 29 shots, average 1.65 s, median 1.62 s, about 35 cuts per minute. Jungle: bursts of 0.17-0.3 s micro-cuts at 1.9-2.6 s and 9.4-11.9 s (flash/crash beats), long 20 s holds otherwise.
- Default for us: **one continuous camera per short** (that is how the 11M one is made), with the camera reaching a new framing every "So you…" beat (~5-7 s).
- The action on screen matches the line being said word for word (he digs when "dig" is said, poles stab on "stab").

## Shots and camera
- Mostly 3/4 elevated side view of the cutaway so above-ground (enemy) and underground (hero) are both in frame: that split is the tension engine `[seen]`.
- Slow drift/push in on the hero during calm lines; low-angle looking up at soldiers when they arrive; top-down shot over water when they search the river; POV up a well shaft.
- Speed: real time; no slow motion; no speed ramps.
- Never shown: gore, the hero caught, text-heavy diagrams, real people.

## Transitions
- Continuous-camera shorts: none; the camera flies to the next framing in ~0.5-1 s.
- Cut-mode shorts: hard cuts only, no dissolves `[measured]`.
- Ending: last frame holds the finished build (wide), video ends on the word "hideout/bunker" with ~0.5 s hold `[seen 1:06-1:08]`.

## Colour and texture
- Natural daylight, saturated sky blue vs warm red-brown earth; underground lit warm orange by a single candle (strong falloff, dark corners) `[seen]`. Night beats: near-black with moon.
- No grain, no letterbox, no vignette beyond the natural lighting. Clean 1080x1920, 60 fps `[measured]`.

## Text on screen
Font: rounded heavy sans, nearest free match **Fredoka Bold** (alt: Baloo 2 ExtraBold) `[seen; unknown exact font, compare letter "g"/"a" side by side to settle]`.

- **Word captions:** ONE word at a time, Title Case, white, ~64 px cap height at 1080 wide, soft dark drop shadow (no thick outline), centred horizontally at **~60% of frame height** `[seen every sheet]`. Keywords in yellow **#F2C230-ish** (nouns that carry the danger or the answer: Soldiers, River, Mud, Snakes, Canteens, Frog), about 1 in 8 words. Swap on each word, no animation visible at 8 fps.
- **Stakes title:** 0-3 s only, top ~5% of frame, 2 lines ALL CAPS, line 1 white, line 2 yellow, thin dark outline + shadow, ~55 px.
- **Callout tag:** yellow rectangle, black ALL-CAPS bold label ("SECRET ENTRANCE", "FALSE BOTTOM", "THATCH ROOF", "NOTCHED POLE"), thin yellow leader line to a yellow dot on the object. Slides in from the side over ~0.25 s, holds ~2 s, on the "This is a ___" line `[seen 0:11-0:14 close/callout]`.
- **Sound-word burst:** yellow 10-12 point starburst, black outline, white tilted ALL-CAPS text with black stroke ("BRRR!", "CLANK!", "ACHOO!", "SPLASH!", "CRASH!", "BZZZ!"). Pops from ~30% to 100% in 0.1 s, holds ~0.6 s with slight tilt, fades over 0.2 s; repeats on each repeated hit (CLANK on every pole stab) `[seen close/burst, close/transition]`. 2-4 per short.
- **Number tag:** big yellow ALL-CAPS number near top ("40 FT") when a size is said `[seen fGH8G4uHG8Q 0:16]`.

## Do and don't
- Do: start talking at 0.0 s with "If you…"; danger visible in frame 0; one continuous cutaway camera; one-word captions; 2-3 callout tags; 2-4 sound-word bursts; cute animal near the end; "Congratulations. You have built a ___."
- Do: keep enemy and hero in the same frame (above vs below ground).
- Don't: talk over 70 s; add a subscribe ask; use stock footage; use flat/cartoon 2D; caption more than one word at a time; use the reference's name, hero design, or any of its titles verbatim.

## Build notes
- **No Blender** `[user 2026-11-26]`: build every short in code, three.js inside HyperFrames. Template: `VIDEO-TOOL/cutaway/waterfall/` (`hf/assets/js/world.js` = the whole 3D film as a function of t; `tools/cues.py` voice words -> cue times; `tools/build.py` writes index.html with captions, stakes title, callouts, bursts; `tools/clean_voice.py` pause trim).
- Renders on the AMD iGPU through ANGLE (hardware WebGL). A snapshot of 13 frames takes about 20 s.
- Cutaway = extruded 2D profile (THREE.ExtrudeGeometry). Three slabs: near bank 0..-1.2 with cave notches and room holes, river channel -1.2..-3, far bank. Caves grow by rebuilding the slab shape. Water = shader planes (falls curtain, pool surface, cut-face gradient), foam and mud = seeded sprites. Assets: Poly Haven CC0 (rock_face_03, forest_ground_04, river_small_rocks, mossy_rock textures; pine_tree_01, fern_02, shrub, rocks, grass glTF; kloofendal puresky HDRI), via `cutaway/tools_ph.py`.
- People: three.js repo Mixamo models (Michelle = hero, Soldier = soldiers, Xbot clips). **Gotchas:** measure model height with `updateMatrixWorld` + `Box3.setFromObject(obj, true)`; retarget clips with q' = dstRest·srcRest⁻¹·q (mode "pre"); do NOT use AnimationMixer for posed people (it skips unchanged bones, so extra rotations pile up): sample tracks with `createInterpolant()` after resetting to rest; pose with `aim(bone, child, dir)` in the character frame (+z forward, +x her left) and `ground()` to the floor. Models face +z at yaw 0.
- Animals: Quaternius German Shepherd (CC-BY 3.0, animated: Walk, Idle_2_HeadLow), Poly by Google river otter (CC-BY 3.0, static): credit both.
- Michelle is stylized (headphones, red glasses). For a realistic hero, the user can download a Mixamo character + clips (digging, kneeling, sleeping) as FBX; three.js FBXLoader reads them.
- Render 1080x1920 at 30 fps. Audio is muxed after the render (voice + real SFX).
- Check with `analyze_reference.py` (0 cuts expected) and loudness -14.5 LUFS.
