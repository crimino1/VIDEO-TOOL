TITLE
What If Sperm Whales Hunted Like Orcas?

DESCRIPTION
The sperm whale has the biggest brain on Earth, a head that can sink a ship, families, dialects and culture. So why doesn't it hunt like an orca? We rewrite evolution to find out what would happen to the ocean if it did.

The animated scenes are illustrations made for this video from NOAA whale paintings, not real footage. The scenario is speculative.
