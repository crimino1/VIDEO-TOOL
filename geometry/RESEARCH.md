## Real problems (only things to act on)
1. 2:28-2:31: the two rules shown are Euclid's **Postulates 1 and 3**, not 1 and 2 (graphics must not say "Postulates 1 and 2").
2. 13:18: "a geometry like this" for the Earth: sphere is a different non-Euclidean geometry from Bolyai's. Visuals: do not show a hyperbolic saddle as "the Earth".
3. 11:24: "hired professional walkers (bematists)" is traditional but not documented for this leg. Soft-word it if the voice can be re-recorded; otherwise keep and avoid on-screen text claiming it.
4. Nothing is WRONG. The pizza claim (an eighth) is exact.

## Exact spellings and dates
- Farkas Bolyai (1775-1856), Janos Bolyai (1802-1860; Janos with an acute accent on the a). ASR "Boljai/Bolia" -> **Bolyai**.
- Farkas's warning letter: 1820. Janos's "strange new universe" letter: 3 November 1823. Appendix published 1832 in Farkas's *Tentamen*.
- Nikolai Ivanovich **Lobachevsky** (1792-1856), Kazan; first public lecture 1826.
- Carl Friedrich **Gauss** (1777-1855). The narration's "Karl Friedrich Gauss" should be "Carl".
- **Euclid's** (apostrophe), Elements; 300 BC approx.
- **Herodotus**, Histories 2.109; c. 484-425 BC.
- **Baudhayana** Sulbasutra (Sulba Sutra), c. 800-500 BC.
- **Aryabhata** (Aryabhata I, born 476 CE), Aryabhatiya 499 CE, Ganitapada verse 10, "asanna".
- **Plimpton 322** (c. 1800 BC, Columbia University), **YBC 7289** (c. 1800-1650 BC, Yale Babylonian Collection).
- **Archimedes** (c. 287-212 BC), *Measurement of a Circle*, 96-gon: 3 10/71 < pi < 3 1/7.
- **Eratosthenes** of Cyrene (c. 276-194 BC); source of the story: Cleomedes.
- **Galileo**, *Discorsi e dimostrazioni matematiche intorno a due nuove scienze*, 1638.
- **Thomas Hales**, Kepler-conjecture proof 1998; honeycomb proof 1999 (published 2001).
- **Einstein**, general relativity, 25 Nov 1915.
- ASR fix: "1.50th" = 1/50th.

## Portraits
No authentic portraits exist of **Euclid**, **Eratosthenes** or **Aryabhata** (all modern or later imaginations). **Janos Bolyai**: no authentic portrait exists. The common image (on the 1960 Hungarian stamp and in encyclopedias) is considered **unauthentic** (MacTutor, Wikipedia), so it was **not downloaded**. Use his signature, the *Appendix* title page or his house instead, or a clearly labelled "artist's impression".
The Commons file used for Eratosthenes is a later imaginary depiction. The Archimedes painting (Fetti, 1620) is an artist's idea; the Herodotus bust is a Roman copy of a Greek original; the Pythagoras bust is a Roman-era portrait of uncertain identity. Hales: no CC-licensed portrait found, not downloaded.
