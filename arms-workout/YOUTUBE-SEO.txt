TITLE
10 Minute Arm Workout for Seniors | Tone Flabby Arms Sitting Down (No Weights)

(shorter option: Seated Arm Workout for Seniors | 10 Min, No Weights)

DESCRIPTION
Tone and strengthen your arms from a chair with this 10-minute follow-along workout for seniors and beginners. Nine seated moves for your biceps, triceps, shoulders and upper back. No weights needed, or hold two water bottles to make it harder.

An on-screen arm meter shows when to work and when to ease off, and a timer counts every set.

Each exercise: 45 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

EXERCISES
0:00 Intro: why strong arms matter
0:30 1. Arm Circles
1:30 2. Seated Biceps Curl
2:30 3. Seated Lateral Raise
3:30 4. Seated Overhead Press
4:30 5. Triceps Extension
5:30 6. Seated Chest Press
6:30 7. Seated Front Raise
7:30 8. Seated Reverse Fly
8:30 9. Seated Punches
9:30 Workout complete!

Repeat this workout 3 times a week. Sit tall on a sturdy chair without wheels. Breathe out on the effort, never hold your breath, and stop if anything hurts. If you have shoulder problems or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
