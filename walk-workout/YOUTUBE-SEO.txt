TITLE
Indoor Walking Workout for Seniors | 10 Minutes, Low Impact, No Equipment

(shorter option: 10 Minute Walk at Home for Seniors | Low Impact)

DESCRIPTION
Walk at home with this easy 10-minute low-impact walking workout for seniors and beginners. Nine simple standing moves (walking in place, step touches, heel taps, marches and more) to raise your energy, get your heart pumping and burn some calories, whatever the weather. No equipment and very little space needed.

An on-screen meter shows which leg is working, and a timer counts every set.

Each move: 45 seconds, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

MOVES
0:00 Intro: why walking matters
0:30 1. Walk in Place
1:30 2. Step Touch
2:30 3. Heel Taps
3:30 4. March with Arms Up
4:30 5. Front & Side Toe Taps
5:30 6. Walk with Punches
6:30 7. Standing Hamstring Curls
7:30 8. Slow High March
8:30 9. Cool-Down Sway
9:30 Workout complete!

Try to walk every day. Wear supportive shoes, clear a little space, and keep a chair nearby to hold if you need it. You should be able to talk while you move; slow down if you get out of breath. If you have heart problems or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
