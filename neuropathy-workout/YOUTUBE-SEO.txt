DESCRIPTION
Eight gentle exercises for numb, tingling or stiff feet, made for seniors and beginners. Two are done seated, three standing and three lying on a mat. They get your feet, ankles and legs moving and your blood flowing, in about 8 minutes with no equipment.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The working muscles glow, an on-screen foot meter shows when to work, and a timer counts every move: 40 seconds of work, then 12 seconds to see the next move.

These exercises can help circulation, ankle movement and balance. They do not cure neuropathy, so keep up with your doctor's care and check your feet every day for cuts or sores.

Hold a sturdy chair for the standing moves if your balance is unsteady. Get down to the mat and back up slowly, rolling onto your side to stand. Stop if you feel sharp pain. If you have diabetes, open wounds on your feet or very poor feeling in them, ask your doctor or podiatrist first.
