DESCRIPTION
A 10-minute follow-along chair workout for men over 50. No equipment, no floor work, just a sturdy chair. Ten simple moves for your legs, arms, chest, shoulders and core, with the working muscles shown in red so you know exactly what each exercise trains.

Each exercise: 42 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.
