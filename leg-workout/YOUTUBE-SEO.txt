TITLE
Top 9 Leg Strengthening Exercises for Seniors | 10 Minute Chair Workout to Walk Better & Prevent Falls

(shorter option: Leg Strength Exercises for Seniors | 10 Min Chair Workout)

DESCRIPTION
The most important leg exercises for seniors, in one 10-minute follow-along chair workout. Strong legs help you walk farther, climb stairs, get up from a chair with ease and keep your balance. Seven moves are done sitting down and two standing in front of the chair. No equipment, just a sturdy chair.

An on-screen leg meter shows which leg is working and when to ease off, and a timer counts every set.

Each exercise: 45 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

EXERCISES
0:00 Intro: why strong legs matter
0:30 1. Seated March (warm-up)
1:30 2. Seated Leg Extension
2:30 3. Seated Knee Raise
3:30 4. Seated Knee Opener
4:30 5. Seated Leg Raise
5:30 6. Standing Calf Raise
6:30 7. Standing Side Leg Raise
7:30 8. Seated Flutter Kicks
8:30 9. Sit to Stand
9:30 Workout complete!

Repeat this workout 3 times a week. Use a sturdy chair without wheels and wear non-slip shoes. For the standing moves, hold the back of the chair if you feel unsteady. Breathe out on the effort and stop if anything hurts. If you have joint problems or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
