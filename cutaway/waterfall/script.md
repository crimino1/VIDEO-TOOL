## Voiceover (as recorded)

If you hide behind a waterfall to escape soldiers, you have a hiding spot. But they can see your shadow right through the water. So you dig a cave into the cliff behind it, and now the only door is a wall of falling water. This is a secret entrance. But the fresh dirt turns the pool brown. So you dump it at night, and the falls carry it far downstream. But then they bring dogs. So you only come and go through the falls, and the spray washes your scent away. But inside, the air turns stale. So you chip a crack through the rock, all the way up to the ferns on top. This is your air vent. But one night, the soldiers camp beside the pool, just to listen for you. So you dig deeper, right under the loudest part of the falls. And that's not a mistake. A waterfall is louder than a busy street, so they can sit there all night and hear nothing. Add a bed of moss, a candle, and an otter who keeps stealing your fish. Congratulations. You have built a secret waterfall hideout.
