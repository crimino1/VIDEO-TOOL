Calculus 1 Full Course, Episode 1: Functions, the warm-up before limits, derivatives and integrals. If you understand functions, calculus becomes easy. In 17 minutes you'll learn what a function really is, how to read its graph, domain and range, the main families of functions, composition f(g(x)) and inverse functions, with 5 practice questions solved step by step.

This series covers the full Calculus 1 / AP Calculus AB syllabus, step by step, from the basics to the final exam.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- What a function is: one input, exactly one output
- Function notation f(x), and why f(x) does not mean f times x
- Graphing a function and the vertical line test
- Domain and range, and the two troublemakers: dividing by zero and square roots of negatives
- Linear (slope and y-intercept), quadratic, polynomial, rational (asymptotes), exponential (e^x), logarithmic (ln x) and trig functions
- Composition of functions, inside first (the key to the chain rule)
- Inverse functions: the 3 steps, the horizontal line test, e^x and ln x
