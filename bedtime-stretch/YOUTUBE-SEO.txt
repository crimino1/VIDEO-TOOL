DESCRIPTION
A calm 10-minute stretch routine you can do lying in bed, made for seniors and beginners over 60. Ten slow moves loosen a stiff back, hips and legs, and the slow breathing helps your body settle for sleep.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The muscles being stretched glow so you can see what each move targets, an on-screen meter shows when to stretch and when to relax, and a gentle timer counts every move: 40 seconds of stretching, then 15 seconds to see the next one.

Do it every night, slowly, and never push into pain. A firm mattress works best.
