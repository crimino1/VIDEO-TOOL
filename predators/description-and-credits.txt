TITLE
Every Deep Sea Predator Explained In 9 Minutes

DESCRIPTION
No light, almost no food, nowhere to hide: so nearly everything down there became a hunter. From the last trace of sunlight to the bottom of the deepest trench, meet every major deep-sea predator: lights that lure, jaws that launch out of the face, stomachs that stretch around prey twice the hunter's size, and even sponges that hunt.

The animated animals are made from real photographs of each species; the behaviour shown follows what scientists have observed and filmed.
