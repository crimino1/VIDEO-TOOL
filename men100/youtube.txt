Could 100 Unarmed Men Beat a T. rex? (Every Prehistoric Predator Ranked)

DESCRIPTION
Could 100 ordinary men beat every predator that ever lived? Not just one - all of them, one at a time.

We put 100 average, untrained, unarmed men on open ground against the deadliest animals in Earth's history: Velociraptor, the dire wolf, the terror bird Kelenken, the saber-toothed cat Smilodon, Utahraptor, the short-faced bear, Titanoboa, Tyrannosaurus rex, Giganotosaurus, Spinosaurus, Deinosuchus, Mosasaurus and Megalodon. Every size, weight and bite force comes from fossils and published research; the fights are imagined. Each predator is ranked on a tier list from "Easy Win" to "Not Even Close".

Then we run the experiment again, honestly. Real Stone Age hunters had spears, fire and a plan - and the fossil record shows they really did kill cave lions and mammoths, from the 48,000-year-old Siegsdorf cave lion to the Naco mammoth with eight Clovis points in its ribs. Could armed humans stand a real chance against a T. rex?

Watch to the end for the final tier list. Do you agree? Tell us in the comments which predator you think would win.
