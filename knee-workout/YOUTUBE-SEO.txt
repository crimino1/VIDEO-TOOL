TITLE
Knee Pain Relief Exercises for Seniors | Strengthen Weak Knees in a Chair (10 Min)

(shorter option: Knee Strengthening Exercises for Seniors | 10 Min Chair Routine)

DESCRIPTION
Gentle exercises to strengthen the muscles around your knees, ease stiffness and make everyday moves like standing up and climbing stairs easier. A calm 10-minute follow-along routine for seniors and beginners: six exercises sitting down, three standing in front of the chair. No equipment, just a sturdy chair and a pillow.

An on-screen meter shows which leg is working, and a timer counts every set.

Each exercise: 45 seconds, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

8:30 9. Sit to Stand
9:30 Routine complete!

Do this routine every other day. Move slowly and stay in a comfortable range: mild effort is fine, sharp pain is not. If you have had knee surgery, a recent injury, or swelling that does not go away, ask your doctor or physiotherapist which exercises are right for you.
