DESCRIPTION
Five simple exercises in the slow, steady Japanese style that together work almost every major muscle in your body: legs, glutes, core, chest, arms and back. We do each move twice, so it's 10 minutes in total, made for seniors and beginners over 60.

The five moves: slow squat, chair push-up, glute bridge, bird dog and heel raises.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The working muscles glow, an on-screen meter shows when to work, and a timer counts every set: 40 seconds of work, then 15 seconds to see the next move. Slow beats fast, so take four seconds down and four seconds up on the squat.
