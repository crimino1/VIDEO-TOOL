TITLE
Balance Exercises for Seniors | 10 Minute Routine to Prevent Falls at Home

(shorter option: 10 Min Balance Exercises for Seniors | Prevent Falls)

DESCRIPTION
A gentle 10-minute follow-along balance workout for seniors and beginners. Nine simple standing exercises that help you stay steady on your feet, walk with confidence and lower your risk of falling. All you need is a sturdy chair to hold on to.

An on-screen meter shows which leg is working, and a timer counts every set.

Each exercise: 45 seconds of work, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

EXERCISES
0:00 Intro: why balance matters
0:30 1. Side-to-Side Weight Shift
1:30 2. Standing March
2:30 3. Heel-Toe Rock
3:30 4. Single-Leg Stand
4:30 5. Standing Side Leg Raise
5:30 6. Standing Back Kick
6:30 7. Front & Side Toe Taps
7:30 8. Heel-to-Toe Stand
8:30 9. Sit to Stand
9:30 Workout complete!

Practise this every day. Stand in front of a sturdy chair or next to a counter and hold on whenever you need to. Wear non-slip shoes. Stop if you feel dizzy or anything hurts. If you have had falls, joint problems or a medical condition, check with your doctor or physiotherapist first.
