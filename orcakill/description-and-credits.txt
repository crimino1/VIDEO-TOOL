TITLE
Orcas Kill These Animals and Never Eat Them

DESCRIPTION
There are only 76 Southern Resident killer whales left, and they are starving. Yet for more than 60 years these same hungry orcas have chased down harbour porpoises, killed them, and swum away without taking a single bite, while the Bigg's orcas next door eat porpoises all the time. Is it a game, training, or something much sadder? Scientists have three theories, and the third one changes how you see the whole thing.

The animation is drawn for this video; the behaviour shown follows what researchers have observed and recorded.
