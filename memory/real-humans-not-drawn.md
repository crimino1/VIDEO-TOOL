On 2026-11-22 the user called the drawn men in [[men100-project]] "very bad" and said: "use real human visual like animals".

**Why:** drawn figures clash with photoreal animal cut-outs and look cheap.

**How to apply:**
- In any realistic or cut-out style, use full-body Pexels photos of people. Search for "full body white background", cut them out with `cut.py`, and puppet them as whole pieces: bob, lean, rotate, swap poses.
- Keep the people small or in groups, not close-ups of one face.
- This is separate from the "no real human" rule for the user themself in shorts ([[unseen-kingdom-2-style]]), which means the user does not appear on camera.
