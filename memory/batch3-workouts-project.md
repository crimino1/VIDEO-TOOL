On 2026-11-12 the user asked for three videos (knee pain, neck & shoulders, indoor walking) in one go, with no questions. They gave three rules: "enhance model, more good", "don't slop colour in editing", and a separate background for each video. Folders: knee-workout, neck-workout, walk-workout. Outputs: done\*-workout-final.mp4.

**Model upgrade**
- chair-workout/blender/um_look.py turns um_thin.blend into um_look.blend.
- It paints point attributes from the head geometry: hair, brow, sclera, iris, lips. face_probe.py prints the depth map that located them.
- Face landmarks (z): hairline about 1.698 at the front (lower at the sides and back), brow ridge about 1.659, eyes at about 1.632 / x ±0.033, nose tip about 1.60, lips about 1.57.
- Material: skin tone varied with noise, a pore bump, lower specular, a little sheen, softer baked AO (removes the dark specks), and hair coloured through noise.
- render_ex.py now defaults to VIEW = AgX / "AgX - Punchy" / exposure −0.6 (was Standard / Medium High Contrast / −0.9). LOOK_CAM env sets a close-up test camera.
- From now on, render every workout from um_look.blend.

**Calm palette**
- Generated by scratchpad make_builds3.py.
- One muted accent per video: knee ochre #D9A441, neck sage #8DB59A, walk terracotta #D7835F.
- Off-white #F4F1EA text, dark glass panels rgba(22,24,28,.82) with a 1px border.
- No glows, no radial meter glow, no rainbow wipe, no bounce eases (back.out swapped for power3.out), no bright green or red.
- The countdown is a dark disc with an accent ring.

**Poses and media**
- 16 new poses in chair_ex.py: the KNEE, NECK and WALK lists, plus a neck() helper that sets neck_01 and head with roll / yaw.
- Neck meter: trapezius segments, with per-row dx in the rows tuple.
- Backgrounds (Pexels, Max Vakhtbovych): beige_4 brick room (knee), beige_7 beige room with curtains (neck), beige_6 wood-wall lounge (walk).
- Voice: 3,543 characters.
- Music: Jesse Keller / Bane Djakovic / Stellar Art Wars (knee), Ehma piano (neck), Neon NiteClub / Felixjd (walk).

**Why:** the user judged the earlier figure too mannequin-like and the editing colours too loud.
**How to apply:** keep um_look.blend and the calm palette as the channel default. Backgrounds used so far: lawn_6, studio_2, gym_0, living_3, yoga_2, loftw_3, beige_4, beige_7, beige_6. Related: [[three-workouts-project]], [[health-workout-style]], [[health-voice-api]].
