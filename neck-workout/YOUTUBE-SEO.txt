TITLE
Neck and Shoulder Pain Relief | 10 Minute Seated Stretches for Seniors

(shorter option: Seated Neck & Shoulder Stretches | 10 Min Relief)

DESCRIPTION
Release tension in your neck and shoulders with this calm 10-minute follow-along stretching routine. Nine gentle seated stretches for stiff necks, tight shoulders and the hunched posture that comes from phones, desks and long sitting. Good for seniors, beginners and office workers. No equipment needed.

An on-screen meter shows which side you are stretching, and a timer counts every set.

Each stretch: 45 seconds, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Stretch every day. Move slowly, breathe, and only stretch to a gentle pull. Never force your neck. If you feel dizziness, numbness, tingling down your arms, or sharp pain, stop and talk to your doctor.
