TITLE
Why You'd Never Want To Meet A Colossal Squid

DESCRIPTION
2,000 metres under the Southern Ocean, an eye the size of a dinner plate is watching. This is the colossal squid: the heaviest invertebrate on Earth, armed with swivelling hooks and a razor beak, and almost never seen alive.

Animation: the 3D squid, sperm whale and submarine scenes and the diagrams are computer-made illustrations for this video, not real footage.
