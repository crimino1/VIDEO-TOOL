DESCRIPTION
Gentle seated sciatica stretches for seniors to ease sciatic nerve pain, tight hips and lower back pain. Follow along from a chair in this calm 10-minute routine: 10 stretches that open the hips, release the glutes and piriformis, and gently glide the sciatic nerve. No equipment, no getting down on the floor.

An on-screen meter shows which side you are working, and a timer counts every stretch: 45 seconds of stretching, then 15 seconds of rest while the next move is shown.

Good for: sciatica, piriformis tightness, hip pain, stiff lower back, beginners, and older adults over 50, 60 or 70 who need gentle chair stretches.

Stretch every day for the best results. Move slowly and only stretch to a gentle pull. Ease off if you feel tingling, and stop if pain spreads down your leg or gets worse. Sudden weakness, numbness, or loss of bladder control needs a doctor straight away. If you have a diagnosed back or hip condition, ask your doctor or physiotherapist first.
