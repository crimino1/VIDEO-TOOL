TITLE
The Only Animal Orcas Won't Hunt: EXCEPT THIS

DESCRIPTION
Orcas eat stingrays, whale sharks, great white sharks, swimming deer and even blue whales. They share the water with surfers, divers and kayakers every day, yet there is not a single confirmed case of a wild orca hunting a human. Why? Four theories, from family food traditions and echolocation to simple energy maths, and the two times orcas suddenly changed what they hunt.

The animated orcas are made from real photographs; the behaviour shown follows what researchers have observed and filmed.
