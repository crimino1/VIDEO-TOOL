TITLE
The Great White Shark Has One Weakness. Orcas Found It

DESCRIPTION
October 1997, Farallon Islands: a female orca holds a great white shark upside down until it goes limp, then swims off with its liver. Every other great white in the area disappears. Years later, in South Africa, two orcas named Port and Starboard empty the world's most famous white shark coast. This is the story of the great white's hidden weakness: tonic immobility, a body that has to keep moving to breathe, and a giant oily liver worth the risk.

The animation is drawn for this video; the behaviour shown follows what researchers have observed and filmed.
