## Hook (first 30 seconds)
Story: a hard opening line with no greeting. One stock party clip with "JUST 3 WEEKS AGO..." `[seen 0:00]`, then one proof per sentence: headline, CEOs, chart, layoffs, each with a joke sticker. The topic's real turn comes at about 0:45 `[seen]`.
Round-up: "AI never sleeps, and this week has been insane", then a list of the week's biggest stories over their real launch pages `[seen 0:00-0:45]`. Do not use that slogan; AI Equator needs its own.

## Video structure
Story: cold open, what happened, explain it with one built diagram, who loses and why it's funny, a date card, sponsor, then the sign-off "thanks for watching, I'll see you in the next one" `[seen]`.
Round-up: hook list, then one block per release (launch page, demo, paper or GitHub, benchmark, one comment), a chapter on the big story, newsletter and subscribe `[seen]`.

## Shots and camera
- Documents float on the stage, usually 60 to 70% of frame width, often with a second one overlapping at about 5 to 8 degrees `[seen]`.
- Long documents slowly scroll, or cut in to the highlighted line `[seen 3:40]`.
- People stay as talking clips with a name plate, or as head-and-shoulders cutouts entering from an edge on top of a document `[seen]`.
- A vertical clip sits in the middle with dark sides, and is never stretched. Keep everything else strictly 16:9 `[seen in reference 3]`.

## Transitions
- **Default:** a hard cut `[seen]`.
- **Push:** the new card slides in from the side and pushes the old shot out, about 0.4 s, ease-out `[seen 0:09.7-0:10.4]`.
- **Scan-line glitch:** picture tears horizontally for about 0.3 s, used to change subject `[seen 1:18]`.
- **Pixel burn:** blocky dissolve or pixelated fire, on "it died" or "it broke" moments `[seen, reference 2 at 0:09]`.
- **Spiral melt:** liquid distortion into the next shot, rare `[seen 0:44]`.
- Round-up uses almost only hard cuts `[seen]`.

## Colour and texture
- Stage: near-black `#0E0C0D` in Stories `[measured]`, pure black or the page's own background in a Round-up `[measured]`.
- Documents stay as they really look, mostly white, and pop hard against the stage `[seen]`.
- Highlight: orange `#FA9432` over document text `[measured]`, some lime green and blue selection `[seen]`.
- Alarm red `#D52243` for stamped words `[measured]`.
- No grain, grade or vignette `[seen]`. Clips keep their source colour.

## Text on screen
- **Big caption:** chunky rounded display type, fill `#F8F3D9`, thick dark stroke `#0F0C0D` `[measured colours]`, centred, about 110 px at 1080p `[seen 0:02]`. All caps, 2 to 5 words. It pops on with no slide.
- **Sarcastic subtitle:** the same font at about 56 px, bottom third, one line `[seen 0:12]`.
- **Word swap:** a word is struck through in red with a yellow replacement on top `[seen 0:27]`.
- **Name plate:** cream box `#ECEDE5` with dark stroke, heavy dark type, bottom of frame `[seen, reference 2 at 0:05]`.
- **Red stamp label:** cream type on a `#D52243` bar for quoted jargon `[seen 2:05]`.
- **Date card:** channel logo and date in red, one short hold `[seen]`.
- **Round-up:** almost no added text. The real page does the talking `[seen]`.

**Font:** the references use their own custom type. For AI Equator use Archivo Black, which is bundled `[proposal]`.

## Graphics
- **Built diagram:** rounded colour boxes, thin arrows, small mono labels, growing one link per sentence on the black stage `[seen 1:50-2:05]`. Build these in HyperFrames.
- **Stickers:** company logos, cutout heads, products, hand-drawn cream arrows; about 2 to 5 at once, slightly rotated, landing in under 0.2 s `[seen]`.
- **Pointer emoji** or a cyan ring on the value that matters in a chart `[seen 1:50, reference 3 at 0:13]`.
- **Pixel-red numbers** with a dark stroke for years and scores `[seen, reference 2 at 0:06]`.

## Do and don't
Do:
- Show the source for every claim, with outlet and date readable.
- Highlight the exact line being read.
- Keep the stage dark, and the documents real.
- Credit every clip from X or another person on screen with their @handle, and link it in the description.

Don't:
- Copy the Code Report logo, its date card, its custom font, "This has been the Code Report", or the "AI never sleeps" slogan. Viewers must not mistake AI Equator for those channels, so its colours and logo need their own touch.
- Fake a real person's words, tweet, article or benchmark. A joke must read as a joke.
- Make realistic fake images or videos of real people. The references do use face-swaps and crude captions; leave those out unless the user decides otherwise, as they risk YouTube strikes and legal complaints.
- Stretch a clip or mix aspect ratios beyond the centred vertical clip.
- Show a stock price or claim without checking it against the primary source first.

## Build notes
- **Master:** HyperFrames at 1920x1080, 30 fps. Set up like VIDEO-TOOL\schrodinger, with a shots file and a build script.
- **Paper or article shot:** `node ai-equator\tools\capture.mjs shot <url> out.png --highlight="exact words"`. Rendered at double pixel density, so zooms stay sharp. Tested on arXiv.
- **Website recording:** `capture.mjs scroll <url> out.mp4 --seconds=8 --px=1400`. It renders frame by frame, so it is perfectly smooth. Tested on Hugging Face.
- **Tweet card:** `capture.mjs tweet <id> out.png`, dark theme, transparent corners, from X's official embed. Tested. Direct x.com pages can show a login wall.
- **Benchmarks:** the same shot or scroll tool on Artificial Analysis, LMArena and each lab's own page. Tested on Artificial Analysis. Use a real page with a highlight, and never retype numbers into a fake table.
- **Demos people built:** find posts on X, then `.venv\Scripts\yt-dlp.exe <post url>`. Tested by looking up two real Seedance posts without downloading. Also works for YouTube. Company demos come from the launch page.
- **Sign-ins:** a site that needs a login gets captured only after the user signs in, in their own browser.
- **Cutouts:** `npx hyperframes remove-background` on a photo. Not yet tested here.
- **Rights:** other people's clips and screenshots are used for commentary and news, with credit, kept short. This reduces but does not remove claim risk. Keep a source list per video for the description.

- **Test edit:** VIDEO-TOOL\ai-equator\test60 (build.py times every beat to a word from words.json) is the project to copy. Rendered 2026-11-07, 61 s, about 1.5 min to render.
- **Fact check found live:** the Artificial Analysis page showed 60 where Google's post said 59. Check live pages against the script before recording the voice.
- **Levels:** normalise the voice before the mix. In the test, voice about -16 LUFS, bed about 15 dB under it, impacts at 0.2 to 0.3 volume.
