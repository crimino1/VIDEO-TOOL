TITLE
Why Orcas Never Hunt Alone

DESCRIPTION
Five orcas swim at an ice floe in a perfect line, dive together and wash a seal into the sea with a wave. The ocean's most powerful predator could hunt alone, yet it almost never does. The real reason has less to do with hunting than with how orcas live: grandmothers who remember, sons who never leave, and hunting techniques taught from one generation to the next.

The animated orcas are made from real photographs; the behaviour shown follows what researchers have observed and filmed.
