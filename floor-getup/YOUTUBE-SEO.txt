DESCRIPTION
Being able to get up off the floor keeps you safe and independent. This 10-minute routine for seniors over 60 builds the strength you need for it, one step at a time, then practises the whole get-up: from lying on your back, to your side, onto hands and knees, into a half-kneel, and up to standing.

Follow along with the 3D trainer. The working muscles glow, an on-screen strength meter shows when to work, and a timer counts every set: 40 seconds of work, then 15 seconds to see the next move.

Practise on a mat with a sturdy chair within reach. Go slowly, and use the chair for support whenever you need it.

If kneeling hurts, put a folded towel under your knees. Stop if you feel pain or get dizzy. If you have had a recent fall, a joint replacement, osteoporosis or heart problems, ask your doctor or physiotherapist first. If you fall and cannot get up, call for help.
