# EP3 review — Why AI Is Making Programmers Worse (8:57)

## What's in it
- 85 shots: 45 new pixel scenes (6 builders: replit / vibe / cost / speed / skill / habits), plus EP2's logo sting, section backdrop and outro.
- Real internet material (user's request):
  - Jason Lemkin's real X chat screenshots ("I deleted the entire database…", "I panicked", "95 out of 100").
  - His rollback post, quoted word for word.
  - Karpathy's original "vibe coding" post and his Feb 2026 retrospective, with the "throwaway weekend projects" line highlighted.
  - A Karpathy on-stage clip (Sequoia "AI Ascent") and Lemkin's public profile photo, each with a name tag.
  - The METR and Anthropic study pages, recorded and cropped (chart, caveats table, results paragraph, interaction-modes chart).
- Stock: 7 Pixabay 1080p/4K clips: server racks, a man at a laptop, a phone, a car with GPS (cropped to hide the car logo), a calculator, a pilot in the cockpit, and a cockpit landing view.
- Sound:
  - Voice at -14 LUFS. 102 soft Pixabay SFX, measured at 24 dB under the voice in loudness and 17 dB under at peaks. No music.
  - Final mix at -14.0 LUFS, -1.3 dBTP.

## Fixed during review
- The captured rollback post showed the parent post in the thread, so it is now a quote card with the exact post text.
- Captions that collided with scene labels were moved or removed ("Does it matter?", "Not everyone…", "Same tool…", "1. Don't ship…").
- Mascot shots now sit over a dimmed scene instead of bare starfield.
- The freeze scene's ALL-CAPS line started too late to finish typing, so it is now cued to "typed".
- The survey answer was cued to "went" and the chart cut moved after it.
- "How I stay out of it" shot added before the 3-habits card, so the card no longer holds 6 s.

## Weakest spots
- Lemkin's profile photo has an AI-style code overlay; it is cropped to his face and kept small.
- The `trap` and `two_groups` characters are small; `iceberg` is the plainest scene.
- `db_gone` counters show in-between numbers while ticking (1,193… 469…), which reads as a counter, not a claim.
