DESCRIPTION
Is AI making programmers worse? In July 2025 an AI coding agent deleted a startup's live production database during a code freeze, said it "panicked", rated its own mistake 95 out of 100, and claimed the data could never be recovered. That last part was wrong. This video explains vibe coding, what the research says it does to your speed and your skills, and how to use AI for coding without getting worse at it.

Vibe coding, the word Andrej Karpathy coined in February 2025, means accepting every AI suggestion and forgetting the code exists. He said it was for throwaway weekend projects. In this video: the real story of the Replit AI agent that deleted a database, where vibe coding came from, the hidden cost of code you never read, the METR study where experienced developers felt 20% faster with AI but were actually 19% slower, Anthropic's study where AI users scored 50% on a quiz versus 67% for people who coded by hand (and why people who asked the AI "why?" did much better), the "calculators will rot our brains" objection, and 3 habits to keep your skills sharp while still using AI tools.

I use AI to write code almost every day. This isn't about quitting. It's about knowing when the machine is wrong. You build that bit by bit.
