DESCRIPTION
Is a computer science degree still worth it in 2027, or did AI kill it? New CS grads have 7% unemployment, CS enrollment just dropped 8.4%, and junior developer jobs are down nearly 20% from their peak. But every one of those numbers has a second column, and almost nobody screenshots it.

In this video I break down the real data from the New York Fed, Stanford and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: unemployment vs underemployment, starting pay, why AI hits juniors and not seniors, which tech jobs are growing and which are shrinking, and what a CS degree actually buys you now. Then the honest verdict: who should do it, who should think twice, and the 3 things to do if you're already in.

Should you study computer science in 2027? Let's take it apart, bit by bit.
